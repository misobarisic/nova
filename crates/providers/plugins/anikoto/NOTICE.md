AniKoto provider behavior is adapted from the Anikoto source in
[`yuzono/anime-extensions`](https://github.com/yuzono/anime-extensions),
including its Apache-2.0-licensed request, VRF transformation, MegaPlay source
decryption/token signing, and Mewcdn extraction conventions.
The Nova JavaScript source is an independent port and does not include the
extension's Kotlin implementation. The upstream project license is available
at <https://github.com/yuzono/anime-extensions/blob/master/LICENSE>.
