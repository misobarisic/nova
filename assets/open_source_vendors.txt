ADAPTED APPLICATION CODE
The TMDB recommendation flow in src/app/similar/tmdb.rs is adapted into Rust
from Nuvio Mobile's TmdbService.kt and TmdbMetadataService.kt (ensureTmdbId and
fetchMoreLikeThis), by the Nuvio Mobile contributors, under GPL-3.0.
Source: https://github.com/NuvioMedia/NuvioMobile/tree/324afcffb962f1f7df45c5fa951131acec5a15a7/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/nuvio/app/features/tmdb
Nova adds typed identity checks, bounded transport and addon-compatible IDs.
The full GPL v3 text is included in this catalog's project-license section.

===== Shared AVIF codec attribution (nova-image) =====

AVIF adapter and test fixture attribution

The plane-access and CICP conversion dispatch in src/avif.rs are adapted from
avif-decode 3.0.0 (https://github.com/kornelski/avif-decode).
The independent tests/fixtures/tiny.avif is its 16x16 8-bit 4:4:4 fixture,
encoded using avifenc rather than Reader's ravif encoder.
The known malformed-data regression flips byte 280 of that fixture.
